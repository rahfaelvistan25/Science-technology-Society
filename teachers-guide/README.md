# Teacher's Guide & Faculty Instructional Manual

**Course:** Science, Technology, and Society (STS 101)  
**Module 1 – Lesson 1:** Introduction to the Historical Antecedents of Science and Technology  
**Level:** College Level (MWF Schedule)  
**Suggested Duration:** 60–90 Minutes  

---

## Folder Structure
- `index.html` — The standalone, interactive **Faculty Instructor's Guide Web Application**.

## Contents of the Teacher's Guide
1. **Course Context & Target Competencies:** Pedagogical objectives matrix mapping each section to student assessments.
2. **Classroom Flow & Time Management:** 60–90 minute timetable allocation breakdown.
3. **Student Misconceptions & Corrections:** Addressing common cognitive pitfalls (e.g. "tech = computers only", "science = technology").
4. **Discussion Facilitation Notes:** Socratic probes, pedagogical tips, and expected student takeaways for Discussions 1, 2, and 3.
5. **Classroom Activities Guides & Rubrics:**
   - Activity 1 (Classifier) Master Solution Key.
   - Activity 2 (Group Research on 5 Inventions) 25-Point Grading Rubric.
   - Activity 3 (Technology Through Time Timeline) Guidelines.
6. **Master Assessment Answer Key:**
   - 15 Multiple Choice Questions with full rationales and distracter analysis.
   - 10 Identification Items with accepted variants.
   - 5 Essay Questions with 5-point evaluation rubrics and model student exemplars.
7. **Preparation for Lesson 2:** Epistemological overview of the Copernican, Darwinian, and Freudian Intellectual Revolutions.
