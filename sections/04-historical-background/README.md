# Section 4: Historical Background – Important People, Events, Dates, and Discoveries

## Chronological Overview

| Era / Date | Milestone / Event | Significance & Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **~700,000 BCE** | Lower Paleolithic Acheulean Handaxe | Earliest standardized stone tool shaping by hominins |
| **~130,000+ BCE** | Controlled Use of Fire | Cooking, warmth, nocturnal light, cave defense, early pyro-technology |
| **~10,000–4,000 BCE** | Neolithic Agricultural Revolution | Transition from nomad foraging to farming, animal domestication, permanent cities |
| **~3500–3200 BCE** | Mesopotamian Inventions | Development of the wheel and Sumerian Cuneiform clay script |
| **~2600–2500 BCE** | Egyptian Pyramids & Indus Urban Planning | Pyramid engineering along the Nile; Mohenjo-Daro planned streets and drainage |
| **~624–287 BCE** | Golden Age of Greek Inquiry | Natural philosophy of Thales, Pythagoras, Hippocrates, Aristotle, Archimedes |
| **105 CE** | Chinese Papermaking Improved | Cai Lun standardizes plant-fiber papermaking during Han Dynasty |
| **~5th–7th Century CE** | Indian Numerical System | Invention of Zero and the decimal place-value system (Aryabhata, Brahmagupta) |
| **~9th–11th Century CE** | Chinese Compass, Gunpowder, Printing | Magnetic navigation, chemical pyrotechnics, and movable-type reproduction |
| **1837–1876 CE** | Industrial Telecommunications | Morse Telegraph and Bell Telephone collapse geographic distances |
| **20th–21st Century** | Digital & AI Revolution | Microprocessors, the Internet, mobile computing, and artificial intelligence |

## Key Historical Figures
- **Thales of Miletus (~624–546 BCE):** First Greek philosopher to seek natural rather than mythological causes for natural events.
- **Pythagoras (~570–495 BCE):** Pioneer of geometry, numerical harmonic relationships, and mathematical deductive proofs.
- **Hippocrates (~460–370 BCE):** Father of Western Medicine; detached medicine from superstition, emphasizing clinical observation and ethics.
- **Aristotle (~384–322 BCE):** Systematic natural philosopher across biology, physics, meteorology, and formal logic.
- **Archimedes (~287–212 BCE):** Master engineer and mathematician; formulated laws of the lever and buoyancy (hydrostatics).
- **Cai Lun (50–121 CE):** Han dynasty court official credited with perfecting lightweight paper from bark, hemp, and rags.
- **Indian Mathematician-Astronomers (e.g. Aryabhata, Brahmagupta):** Codified rules of zero, decimal arithmetic, and algebraic methods.
