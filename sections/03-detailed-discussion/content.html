III. Lesson Proper

Topic 1: Understanding Historical Antecedents

1.1 What are Historical Antecedents?

Historical antecedents refer to events, discoveries, ideas, inventions, and developments that happened in the past and influenced what exists today.

1.2 Why Should We Study Historical Antecedents?

Understanding the origins of modern tech, recognizing creativity, learning from mistakes, understanding societal impact, and appreciating scientific progress.

Topic 2: The Meaning of Science and Technology

2.1 What is Science?

Systematic study of the natural world through observation, questioning, hypothesis, experimentation, analysis, and conclusion.

2.2 What is Technology?

Practical application of scientific knowledge to solve human problems.

2.3 Difference Between Science and Technology

Science seeks to understand the world; technology applies that understanding to improve the world.
