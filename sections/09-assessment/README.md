# Section 9: Assessment – Quizzes, Identification, and Essay Questions

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions (15 Items)

1. What does the term *historical antecedents* refer to?
   - A. Future inventions and discoveries.
   - **B. Events and developments from the past that influenced the present.**
   - C. Modern technological applications only.
   - D. Scientific theories that have not been tested.

2. What is the primary purpose of science?
   - A. To manufacture machines.
   - B. To create electronic devices.
   - **C. To understand the natural world through observation and investigation.**
   - D. To develop business products.

3. Which of the following best describes technology?
   - A. The study of historical events.
   - **B. The application of knowledge and skills to solve practical problems.**
   - C. The study of planets only.
   - D. The process of memorizing information.

4. Which early technological development helped humans cook food and stay warm?
   - A. Printing press.
   - B. Electricity.
   - **C. Fire.**
   - D. Telephone.

5. What was one of the most important contributions of the Agricultural Revolution?
   - A. Development of smartphones.
   - **B. Establishment of permanent settlements through food production.**
   - C. Invention of airplanes.
   - D. Development of computers.

6. Which civilization developed the cuneiform writing system?
   - A. Ancient Greece.
   - **B. Mesopotamia.**
   - C. Ancient Rome.
   - D. Ancient Egypt.

7. Which ancient civilization is famous for constructing the pyramids of Giza?
   - A. China.
   - B. India.
   - **C. Egypt.**
   - D. Greece.

8. Who is associated with the Pythagorean theorem?
   - A. Archimedes.
   - **B. Pythagoras.**
   - C. Aristotle.
   - D. Hippocrates.

9. Which civilization contributed significantly to the development of papermaking, the compass, and gunpowder?
   - **A. Ancient China.**
   - B. Ancient Greece.
   - C. Mesopotamia.
   - D. Ancient Egypt.

10. Which mathematical concept was an important contribution of ancient Indian mathematics?
    - A. Steam engine.
    - **B. Zero and the decimal place-value system.**
    - C. Printing press.
    - D. Mechanical clock.

11. What is the relationship between science and technology?
    - A. They are completely unrelated.
    - **B. Science develops knowledge, while technology applies knowledge to practical problems.**
    - C. Science only studies history.
    - D. Technology is always developed before scientific knowledge.

12. What is an example of technology influencing society?
    - A. People observing the stars.
    - **B. Smartphones allowing instant communication.**
    - C. Scientists studying gravity.
    - D. Researchers observing plants.

13. What is the digital divide?
    - A. The difference between ancient and modern computers.
    - **B. The unequal access to digital technologies and internet resources.**
    - C. The process of manufacturing computers.
    - D. The development of programming languages.

14. Which of the following is an example of responsible technology use?
    - A. Sharing passwords with strangers.
    - B. Spreading unverified information.
    - **C. Protecting personal information and verifying online sources.**
    - D. Using AI to submit unverified information as original work.

15. Why is studying the history of science and technology important?
    - **A. It helps us understand how past discoveries influenced modern developments.**
    - B. It prevents future inventions.
    - C. It eliminates the need for scientific research.
    - D. It teaches us to avoid using modern technology.

## Part B: Identification (10 Items)
1. The systematic study of the natural world through observation and experimentation. -> **Science**
2. The practical application of knowledge to solve problems. -> **Technology**
3. The early period characterized by the extensive use of stone tools. -> **Stone Age**
4. The process of transitioning from hunting and gathering to farming and animal domestication. -> **Agricultural Revolution**
5. One of the earliest known writing systems developed by the Sumerians. -> **Cuneiform**
6. The ancient civilization located between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers. -> **Mesopotamia**
7. The Greek mathematician associated with the famous theorem involving right triangles. -> **Pythagoras**
8. The ancient civilization associated with the development of zero and the decimal place-value system. -> **Ancient India**
9. The process of creating new solutions, ideas, or products to address human needs. -> **Innovation**
10. The unequal access to digital devices, internet connectivity, and digital resources. -> **Digital Divide**

## Part C: Essay Questions (3–5 Sentences each)

2. **Explain how early human discoveries such as fire, stone tools, and agriculture influenced the development of civilization.**
3. **Discuss the contributions of any two ancient civilizations to the development of modern science and technology.**
4. **Explain the relationship between science, technology, and society. Give one real-life example.**
5. **In your opinion, does technology always improve human life? Explain your answer using examples.**
