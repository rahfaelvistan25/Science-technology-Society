# Section 2: Learning Objectives – What Students Should Learn

At the end of this lesson, students should be able to:

1. **Define** science, technology, and historical antecedents.
2. **Explain** how science and technology originated and developed throughout human history.
3. **Identify** important early human discoveries and technological innovations.
4. **Describe** the contributions of ancient civilizations to scientific and technological development.
5. **Explain** the relationship between science, technology, and society.
6. **Recognize** the importance of historical discoveries in shaping modern technology.
7. **Demonstrate appreciation** for the role of human curiosity, creativity, and innovation in civilization.
