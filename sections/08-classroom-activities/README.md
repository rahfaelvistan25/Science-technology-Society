# Section 8: Classroom Activities – Individual or Group Exercises

## Activity 1: Science or Technology? (Individual Activity)
**Instructions:** Identify whether each example represents Science, Technology, or Both.

| No. | Example | Answer | Explanation |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Studying how gravity works | **Science** | Investigation of natural physical laws. |
| 2 | Developing a smartphone | **Technology** | Practical engineering application of telecommunications. |
| 3 | Investigating how plants grow | **Science** | Biological inquiry into botany and photosynthesis. |
| 4 | Creating a medical imaging machine | **Technology** | Engineering device to detect health anomalies. |
| 5 | Studying the movement of planets | **Science** | Astronomical observation of celestial mechanics. |
| 6 | Developing an irrigation system | **Technology** | Hydraulic civil engineering for agriculture. |
| 7 | Researching how electricity works | **Science** | Theoretical inquiry into electrons and electromagnetic forces. |
| 8 | Designing an electric vehicle | **Technology** | Application of mechanics, battery chemistry, and electric drive. |
| 9 | Studying the spread of diseases | **Science** | Epidemiological study of viral transmission. |
| 10 | Developing a vaccine | **Both** | Combines biomedical scientific research with biotechnology manufacturing. |

## Activity 2: Historical Inventions and Their Modern Applications (Group Activity)
**Instructions:**
1. Divide the class into five groups.
2. Each group researches one historical invention and explains how it influenced modern technology.

| Group | Historical Invention | Main Focus Area |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| Group 1 | **Fire** | Energy generation, thermal control, and cooking systems |
| Group 2 | **The Wheel** | Transportation, mechanical gearing, and industrial motion |
| Group 3 | **Writing Systems** | Information recording, contracts, telecommunications, and digital code |
| Group 4 | **Irrigation** | Hydraulic engineering, agriculture, and municipal water management |
| Group 5 | **Papermaking** | Information dissemination, education, printing, and paper products |

### Presentation Deliverables:
- Historical background of the invention
- The civilization or people associated with its development
- Its original purpose
- Its impact on human civilization
- Its connection to modern technology
- **Expected Output:** 5–10-minute group presentation with visual aid.

## Activity 3: Technology Through Time (Individual or Pair Activity)
**Instructions:**
Choose one modern technology and trace its historical development:
- Computer
- Smartphone
- Automobile
- Airplane
- Television
- Medical equipment
- Internet
- Agricultural machinery

**Requirement:** Create an illustrated timeline showing at least **5 major historical developments** leading up to the modern form, with explanatory text for each milestone.
